# Devoted Health

> Devoted Health is a technology-enabled Medicare Advantage payer (founded 2017, Waltham, MA) that publishes CMS-required interoperability FHIR R4 APIs: a member Patient Access API (claims, encounters, conditions, medications) and public Provider & Pharmacy Directory and Plan Coverage & Formularies APIs. Member data access uses OAuth 2.0 / OpenID Connect with member consent per the 21st Century Cures Act.

## APIs

- [Patient Access API](https://api.prod.devoted.com/fhir): Member claims, encounters, conditions, and medications (FHIR R4). Requires OAuth 2.0 member authorization.
- [Provider & Pharmacy Directory API](https://fhir.devoted.com/fhir): In-network providers, facilities, and pharmacies (PDEX Plan-Net). Public.
- [Plan Coverage & Formularies API](https://fhir.devoted.com/fhir): Drug formulary information (PDEX US Drug Formulary). Public.
